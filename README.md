# Steelsetu

**The Bridge Between Verified Buyers & Verified Steel Suppliers**

Steelsetu is a B2B iron and steel trading platform for India's steel market, starting in Burrabazar, Kolkata, West Bengal, and expanding to Eastern India and then Pan-India.

- **Domain:** steelsetu.com
- **Operating context:** Singhi Enterprises, Burrabazar, Kolkata
- **PRD Version:** 5.0 — Final Consolidated Blueprint (11 November 2026)

## What Steelsetu Is

One integrated website with four connected product layers:

1. **Directory & Tools** — Verified supplier directory, brand directory, product catalogue, indicative market rates, steel calculators, and educational trade knowledge.
2. **Marketplace & CRM** — Buyer requirements, RFQs, supplier quotations, WhatsApp-first contact, and the Singhi Trade Desk.
3. **Community Hub** — Trade Q&A, polls, events, and focused trade rooms.
4. **Trust & Market Feedback Layer** — Moderated business reviews, market conduct notes, private report channels, right of reply, and appeals.

## Product Development Priority

**Fix the Core → Community v1 → Trust v1 → Marketplace Improvements → Growth & Intelligence**

## Technology Direction

- Next.js (App Router) + React + TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS, mobile-first responsive design
- PostgreSQL (Supabase-compatible), Row Level Security
- Vercel deployment
