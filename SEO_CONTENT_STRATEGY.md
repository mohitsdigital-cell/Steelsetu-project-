# SEO_CONTENT_STRATEGY

## Technical SEO

- Server-render public pages; correct titles and meta descriptions; canonical URLs; XML sitemap; robots.txt; clean URLs; breadcrumbs; Open Graph metadata; image optimisation; fast mobile rendering.
- No broken internal links; correct 301 redirects; correct 404 status codes.
- Do not index thin, duplicate, or private pages. Never use noindex as a substitute for fixing broken public pages or search architecture.
- Monitor Google Search Console, indexing, and Core Web Vitals.

## Directory and Local SEO

Build genuinely useful pages only where real listings exist:

- Steel suppliers in Burrabazar, Kolkata; steel traders in Kolkata.
- TMT suppliers in Kolkata; MS sheet suppliers in Kolkata.
- GI and GP sheet suppliers in Eastern India; roofing sheet suppliers in West Bengal.
- FRP and polycarbonate sheet suppliers; relevant product-city combinations.

No hundreds of thin city pages with copied text. Addresses, service areas, contact details, and operating claims must be accurate and business-approved.

## Content Clusters

1. Steel Basics — TMT grades explained; steel weight calculation; MS vs GI vs GP vs PPGI vs PPGL; sheet thickness and gauge; coil terminology; steel quality documentation.
2. Roofing and Industrial Materials — FRP vs polycarbonate; PUF panel applications; roofing sheet installation; ridge and flashing selection; roofing screw selection; ventilation basics; industrial roof maintenance.
3. Buying and Trade Education — how to prepare an RFQ; what to compare in steel quotations; GST and freight fields to clarify; what to check before dispatch; how to read a material test certificate; how to document a weight discrepancy.
4. Market Education — interpreting indicative rates; factors affecting steel prices; regional market differences; infrastructure and construction demand; green steel developments.

Editorial rules: never publish a market forecast as fact; cite sources and methodology; direct answers near the top; clear headings; tables for comparisons; consistent terminology; author and reviewer identity visible; transparent corrections.

## Structured Data (only where page content supports it)

Organization, LocalBusiness (where appropriate), Product, BreadcrumbList, Article, Person, Event, WebSite and SearchAction. FAQPage only where current search-engine eligibility rules and content support it. Never mark up fake ratings, hidden reviews, invented prices, or content not visible to users.

## E-E-A-T

- Experience: practical trade examples; real product handling and buying considerations; content reviewed by experienced industry participants.
- Expertise: technical review of formulas and specifications; assumptions and units explained; reliable source documents; transparent corrections.
- Authoritativeness: original educational material; authoritative references; genuine industry relationships; accurate contributor attribution.
- Trustworthiness: clear business identity; transparent editorial policy; rate disclaimers; corrections policy; privacy and grievance info; no fabricated testimonials or social proof.

## Editorial Voice

Professional, practical, clear, local-market aware, honest about uncertainty. No hype, income promises, fake urgency, unsupported superlatives, fake market predictions, keyword stuffing, generic AI paragraphs, or guaranteed quality or profit claims.

## CTA Rules (contextual, not interruptive)

Product guide to RFQ; calculator to convert estimate into RFQ; supplier profile to contact supplier; Q&A to ask a trade question; community requirement to convert to RFQ; market guide to browse relevant suppliers.
